Client: Jordan Ellis, DOB February 2, 2012
On May 10, 2022, Jordan was seen at an emergency room in Washington, DC after falling off his bike. 
The X-ray was read as a sprained ankle, and he was sent home. On March 3, 2024, an orthopedist found that he had actually fractured a growth plate. 
Because the fracture was never treated, one leg is now shorter than the other, and he will need corrective surgery.
